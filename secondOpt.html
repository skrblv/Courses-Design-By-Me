// Manifesto

Traditional education is slow, generic, and built for a web that stopped existing a decade ago. The internet rewards the bold, the fast, and the unreasonably detailed. We built NEXUS to train the people who will build what comes next.

Curriculum

Four studios.
One craft.

Every studio runs for a fixed number of weeks, ends in a shipped project, and is graded by working practitioners, not a rubric.

Method

How a cohort
actually runs.

No lectures longer than twenty minutes. No busywork. Four repeating phases, every single week.

01

Immerse

Mon–Tue

You study a real, shipped product built by the studio you're learning that discipline from, and reverse-engineer why every decision was made.

02

Build

Wed–Thu

You build the same class of problem from a blank file, paired with a mentor who has shipped that exact thing at scale before.

03

Critique

Fri

Your work is reviewed live, on a projector, in front of your cohort. No feedback forms. Notes are blunt, specific, and public.

04

Ship

Ongoing

Every project goes live on a real domain with real users watching, because a portfolio piece nobody can visit teaches you nothing.
